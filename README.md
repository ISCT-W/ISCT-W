# Junhao Wei · 韦君豪

**Generative AI · Reliable agents · Trustworthy machine learning**

I hold a PhD in Systems and Control Engineering from **Institute of Science Tokyo** (formerly Tokyo Institute of Technology). I study the security and reliability of AI models and work on agent and multimodal applications, with an interest in turning research ideas into systems that can be inspected and evaluated.

## Research interests

- **Generative AI security and privacy:** language models, multimodal models, diffusion models, and model-merging defenses.
- **Reliable agent applications:** tool use, retrieval-augmented generation, context and memory, evaluation, and failure handling.
- **Trustworthy machine learning:** uncertainty estimation, conformal prediction, interpretability, and evidence-based evaluation.

## Selected research

- **A Single Suffix to Break Them All: Basin-Aware Jailbreaks for Merged Model Families** — accepted to Findings of EMNLP 2026, second author. [Research notes](https://isct-w.github.io/en/blog/research/basin-aware-jailbreak-minmax/)
- **Disrupting Model Merging: A Parameter-Level Defense Without Sacrificing Accuracy** — ICCV 2025, co-first author. PaRaMS studies parameter transformations that preserve a model’s standalone utility while disrupting direct merging. [Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Junhao_Disrupting_Model_Merging_A_Parameter-Level_Defense_Without_Sacrificing_Accuracy_ICCV_2025_paper.html) · [Code](https://github.com/ISCT-W/PaRaMS) · [Explanation](https://isct-w.github.io/en/blog/research/params-model-merging-defense/)
